About Us

Transforming lives, building communities, creating lasting change

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities in Jharkhand through quality education, healthcare, clean water access, and sustainable livelihood programs that foster dignity, opportunity, and hope.

Our Vision

A Jharkhand where every child has access to education, every family has clean water, every person has healthcare, and every community thrives with sustainable development and social justice.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Transparency, Accountability, and Sustainability guide everything we do. We believe in listening to communities and co-creating solutions.

Who We Are

Global Reach (Meraki) is a non-profit organization founded in 2018 with a passion to create meaningful change in rural and urban communities across Jharkhand. We are a team of dedicated social workers, educators, healthcare professionals, and community leaders committed to social justice.

With over 500+ dedicated volunteers and 50+ field staff, we've directly impacted the lives of 25,000+ individuals across 150+ villages in Jharkhand. Our work spans education, healthcare, clean water, women empowerment, and sustainable livelihood development.
