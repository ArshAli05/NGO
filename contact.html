Frequently Asked Questions

How can I make a donation?

You can make a donation through our website using multiple payment methods including card, UPI, or bank transfer. Visit our Donate section for more details.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! Meraki is registered under section 80G of the Income Tax Act. Donations above ₹500 are tax-deductible in India.

How can I volunteer with Meraki?

We welcome volunteers! Please reach out to us via the contact form above or email support@meraki.org with details about your skills and interests.

Where does my donation go?

We maintain complete transparency. 85% of donations go directly to programs, and 15% covers administrative costs. Visit our Impact page for detailed reports.

How often do you provide updates?

We send quarterly impact reports to all donors. You can also subscribe to our newsletter for monthly updates and stories.

Do you have operations in other states?

Currently, we focus exclusively on Jharkhand. However, we're exploring expansion to neighboring states in the next two years.
