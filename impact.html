Our Journey

2015

Foundation Year

Meraki was established with a vision to transform rural Jharkhand through education and healthcare initiatives.

2017

Education Programs Launch

Launched skill development centers in 5 districts, impacting 500+ youth in their first year.

2019

Water & Sanitation Initiative

Constructed 45 water wells and sanitation facilities, reaching 8,500+ people directly.

2021

Women Empowerment Focus

Dedicated programs for women's economic empowerment with 200+ women entrepreneurs established.

2023

Digital Literacy Era

Introduced digital skills training, reaching 3,000+ individuals in remote areas.

2025

Regional Expansion

Expanding operations to 25 districts with 150,000+ lives impacted across Jharkhand.
